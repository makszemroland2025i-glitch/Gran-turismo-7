What is Gran Turismo 7?

Gran Turismo 7 brings together the very best features of the Real Driving Simulator.

Whether you’re a competitive or casual racer, collector, tuner, livery designer or photographer – find your line with a staggering collection of game modes including fan-favourites like GT Campaign, Arcade and Driving School*.

With the reintroduction of the legendary GT Simulation Mode, buy, tune and race your way through a rewarding solo campaign as you unlock new cars and challenges. And if you love going head-to-head with others, hone your skills and compete in the GT Sport Mode.

With over 420 cars available at Brand Central and the Used Car Dealership from day one, Gran Turismo 7 recreates the look and feel of classic motors and bleeding-edge supercars alike in unparalleled detail. Each car handles differently and feels unique as you navigate over 90 track routes in dynamic weather conditions, including classic courses from GT history.

*Internet connection required for most functionality

Gran Turismo 7 showroom

Get a 360-degree view of some of the premiere cars featured in Gran Turismo 7.

Porsche 917 LIVING LEGEND

The Porsche 917 LIVING LEGEND is a hypercar inspired by the legendary Porsche 917 racer, which brought the manufacturer its first overall victory in Le Mans back in 1970. Developed in 2013 by a small team of engineers and designers in Weissach, the 917 LIVING LEGEND was simultaneously a tribute and spiritual successor to the legendary Porsche racer. Under the sheet metal, it featured the latest technology of its day, including lightweight materials such as carbon fibre, which was used for the chassis. Powering the car was a turbocharged 8-cylinder engine that pumped out about 1014ps, while the suspension system, based on the 918 Spyder, provided remarkable handling at all speeds.

How innovative PS5 features enhance Gran Turismo 7

Stunning visuals

Catch sight of the driver in your rear-view mirror, watch the sun glint against the curves of your car’s body, and take stunningly realistic photos with the power of ray tracing on PS5®, with support for 4K and HDR at a targeted 60fps framerate.

Fast loading

The PS5 console’s ultra-high-speed SSD lets you quickly start race events, gather in lobbies, browse your garage and seamlessly navigate GT7’s modes and menus.

Adaptive triggers

Feel the vibration from the anti-lock brake system and wheel spins, and variation in the braking resistance of each different car, all through the adaptive triggers of your DualSense® wireless controller.

Haptic feedback

From the subtle bumps of the tarmac to the grooves of the kerb, feel your position on the road through immersive haptic feedback.

Tempest 3D AudioTech

Sense the position of other cars and drivers on the road with unparalleled clarity through the PS5 console’s 3D Audio with compatible headphones.
